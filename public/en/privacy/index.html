Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the information you provide through the enquiry form on robotics.al.

What information is collected

Only what you type into the form yourself: name, email, business, city or region, facility type, primary challenge, and the optional fields (room count, estimated monthly spend, existing monitoring, role, timeline and extra details).

How it is processed

When you submit the form, the information is received by Netlify, our hosting and form-processing provider, stored in our Netlify account, and we are notified by email. The site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking tools. We do not share the information with others for marketing.

Purpose and basis

The information is used only to respond to your enquiry and to assess interest in a pilot. The basis is your consent, given by ticking the consent box.

Hosting and location

Netlify may process data outside Albania and the European Economic Area. As with any hosting provider, it may technically log visitors’ IP addresses. We do not control those logs.

Your rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your information by writing to the contact below, under the applicable data-protection law.

Retention

Enquiries that do not progress are deleted within 90 days of receipt. Enquiries that progress are kept as long as needed for the discussion, a pilot or legal obligations.
