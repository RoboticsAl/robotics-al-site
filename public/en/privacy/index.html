Privacy notice

This website is published by the Robotics.al initiative. Contact: info@robotics.al.

The enquiry draft

Your answers stay in this page while you prepare and review an email draft. The website does not submit the form, store enquiries in a database or use analytics, cookies or local storage.

Opening your email app transfers the draft to that app. You decide whether to send it. Once sent, the message is handled by your email provider and our email provider. We use received enquiries to discuss your request and potential pilot work. Please avoid sending sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub processes technical information when serving pages, including visitors’ IP addresses. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions about your information

For questions about a received enquiry or a request to correct or delete it, write to info@robotics.al. No automated 90-day deletion service is configured.
